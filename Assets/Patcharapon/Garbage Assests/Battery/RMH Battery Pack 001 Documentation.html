Requires Unity 5.3.2 or higher.

RMH Battery Pack 001.

This is a low-polygon collection of 8 common battery types with unique texture designs, plus an interpretation of Alessandro Volta's Voltaic Pile. Each battery has basic colliders added, and where needed, Mesh colliders have been created (the Volta battery has a collection of Box colliders, though a Mesh collider has also been provided).

Models are all .fbx format, and textures are PNGs (the UV layouts are easily extracted from the .fbx models with compatible 3D CAD software [like Blender]).

All batteries have prefabs and have a consistent scale (some liberties were taken with Volta's Battery). Batteries included are:

9V, 894 triangles, 1024x1024 px, Albedo, AO, Metallic, Normal
A23, 508 triangles, 1024x1024 px, Albedo, AO, Normal
AA, 796 triangles, 1024x1024 px, Albedo, AO, Normal
AAA, 540 triangles, 1024x1024 px, Albedo, AO, Normal
C Type, 764 triangles, 1024x1024 px, Albedo, AO, Normal
CR2032, 284 triangles, 2048x2048 px, Albedo, AO, Normal
D Type, 764 triangles, 1024x1024 px, Albedo, AO, Normal
Digital Camera 05, 380 triangles, 2048x2048 px, Albedo, Metallic, Normal
Volta's Battery, 4345 triangles, 2048x2048 px, Albedo, AO, then a mix of Metallic and Normal
